# Session 02: MCP Servers and Connecting Your Tools

Last session we gave Claude a brain. This session we're giving it HANDS. By the end of this one, Claude won't just know who you are and how you like things done. It'll be able to actually reach into your apps and DO things.

## What's In This Folder

- **student-guide.md** - Your step-by-step walkthrough for connecting tools to Claude Desktop. Follow this during and after class.
- **lesson-plan.md** - The full session structure with talking points and timing.

## What You'll Walk Away With

- At least one external tool connected to Claude Desktop
- Understanding of the three connection methods (Extensions, Plugins, Manual MCP)
- Knowledge of what's available in the MCP ecosystem
- Your CLAUDE.md updated with connected tools
- Security best practices for managing app connections

## Before Class

Make sure you have:

1. Your Claude Desktop project folder from Session 1 (with CLAUDE.md, skills/, and memory/)
2. Access to at least one of these accounts: Google (Calendar/Gmail), Slack, or Notion
